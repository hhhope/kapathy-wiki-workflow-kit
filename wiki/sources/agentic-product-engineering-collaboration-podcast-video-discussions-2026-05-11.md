# Agentic Product Engineering Collaboration / Podcast And Video Discussions

## Purpose

The previous source pool over-weighted articles, reports, product posts, and
engineering blogs. This file adds a dedicated discussion layer: podcasts,
YouTube interviews, long-form operator conversations, and episode transcripts
that discuss how agents change product engineering collaboration, software
engineering work, product/design roles, engineering management, company
operating models, and human judgment.

Discussion-layer count: 36 source leads.

Use this as a source router, not a quotation pack. Podcast pages often include
summaries, show notes, and partial transcripts, but final public claims still
need episode-level listening or transcript capture.

## What This Adds

1. The podcast layer is where role language becomes explicit: PM collapse,
   coder-to-manager, designer-to-prototyper, field engineer as codebase
   navigator, manager as agent orchestrator.
2. Several episodes discuss internal operating systems rather than external
   tools: Intercom's skills/telemetry, Notion's agent platform, OpenAI's
   parallel agents, Thoughtworks AI/works, Gartner's API/platform argument.
3. The strongest people-model sources are not generic trend articles. They are
   operator interviews: Keith Rabois, Cat Wu, Boris Cherny, Sherwin Wu, Brian
   Scanlan, Simon Last, Karpathy, and Marc Brooker.
4. The caution layer also improves: Stack Overflow, Practical AI, Thoughtworks,
   and Software Engineering Radio all push against naive speed-only narratives.
