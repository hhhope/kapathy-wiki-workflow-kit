## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Stripe H5 redesign section alignment

The Stripe AI company evolution H5 SHALL preserve the accepted redesign's
reader-facing chapter structure for strategic causality, domain-model
standardization, and AI-era payment restructuring.

#### Scenario: Reader reviews strategic causality

- **WHEN** a reader reaches the post-timeline strategy chapter
- **THEN** the page SHALL include a visible `哪些战略造就了 Stripe` section
- **AND** it SHALL show strategic choice -> capability accumulation -> later
  product / acquisition -> AI-era reuse as the section's visual logic
- **AND** it SHALL cover developer entry, platform merchants, revenue
  operations, risk infrastructure, global/local gap fill, issuing / wallet /
  entitlement entry, and new financial rails.

#### Scenario: Reader reviews why Stripe keeps doing hard work

- **WHEN** a reader reaches the domain-model and standardization chapter
- **THEN** the page SHALL include a visible `Stripe 为什么坚持做难事：API、规范和领域模型`
  section
- **AND** it SHALL explain that APIs, domain objects, docs, test mode, risk,
  tax, compliance, issuing / entitlement, wallets, and new rails are business
  strategy rather than engineering polish.

#### Scenario: Reader reviews AI-era payment restructuring

- **WHEN** a reader reaches the AI-era payment chapter
- **THEN** the page SHALL present the section around infrastructure pressure,
  transaction relationship, credit relationship, entitlement / credential
  entry, MCP / skills / sandbox / API tooling, AP2 / ACP / MPP / x402
  accountability, and return to Stripe's company path
- **AND** the page SHALL avoid treating keyword presence as sufficient
  structure coverage.

#### Scenario: Repair evidence is recorded

- **WHEN** this repair is completed
- **THEN** a repo-visible drift retro SHALL record the cause and replay check
- **AND** the report gate review SHALL record the repair boundary,
  verification evidence, and non-publication status.
