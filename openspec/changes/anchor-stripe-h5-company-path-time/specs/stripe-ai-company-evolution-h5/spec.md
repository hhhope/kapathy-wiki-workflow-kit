## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Stripe company path chronology

The Stripe AI company evolution H5 SHALL connect company path stages to explicit
time ranges and strategic choices.

#### Scenario: Reader reviews company path

- **WHEN** a reader reaches the company path section
- **THEN** the section heading SHALL show that the path covers 2010-2026
- **AND** each path stage SHALL include a visible time range
- **AND** each stage SHALL explain strategic choice, accumulated capability, and
  resulting position.

#### Scenario: Reader reviews timeline framing

- **WHEN** a reader reaches the timeline section
- **THEN** the heading SHALL be a concrete business-analysis claim rather than
  abstract wording about Stripe embedding deeper
- **AND** the supporting paragraph SHALL explain the stage logic with concrete
  choices and capabilities.
