## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Stripe H5 professional report visual language

The Stripe AI company evolution H5 SHALL use professional report language and
non-decorative visual encodings for its relationship rails, timeline, AI
restructuring section, source quote section, and signal section.

#### Scenario: Reader reviews transaction and credit rails

- **WHEN** a reader reaches the first-screen relationship module
- **THEN** the transaction and credit rails SHALL use professional payment /
  finance terminology rather than casual explanatory sentences
- **AND** the visual SHALL look like an operating-model rail, not two plain text
  cards.

#### Scenario: Reader reviews evidence-pool and volume copy

- **WHEN** a reader opens the first screen
- **THEN** evidence-pool copy SHALL avoid conversational phrases such as
  `不把 1000 条 queue 写成 1000 条结论`
- **AND** the processed-volume title SHALL not imply a source claim that is not
  visible in the section.

#### Scenario: Reader reviews the timeline

- **WHEN** a reader reaches the company timeline
- **THEN** the support copy SHALL state the business-analysis premise compactly
- **AND** the timeline/detail labels SHALL not use unclear labels such as
  `证据维度` without visible encoding support.

#### Scenario: Reader reviews AI payment restructuring

- **WHEN** a reader reaches the AI-era payment restructuring section
- **THEN** the section SHALL use market-structure and payment-industry language
  instead of generic AI commentary
- **AND** the visual SHALL separate demand distribution, credentials, merchant
  systems, settlement rails, and risk/credit control.

#### Scenario: Reader reviews source quotes

- **WHEN** a reader reaches the source quote section
- **THEN** the section SHALL explain why citations appear in the report
- **AND** source quotes SHALL be presented as simple gray quote blocks rather
  than a source-management dashboard.

#### Scenario: Reader reviews signal section

- **WHEN** a reader reaches the signal section
- **THEN** the section SHALL not render as a decorative word cloud
- **AND** it SHALL use grouped evidence lanes or bars that can be scanned
  without relying on clicking.
