## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Reader-facing report language

The Stripe AI company evolution H5 SHALL avoid conversational production
language in visible reader-facing copy.

#### Scenario: Reader scans page copy

- **WHEN** a reader scans the H5
- **THEN** visible section copy uses report-language
- **AND** production/process terms such as `proof`, `lane`, `boundary`,
  `queue`, and `draft` are not visible in reader-facing page copy
- **AND** repeated negative framing such as `不是` and `不能证明` is avoided in
  primary visible copy
