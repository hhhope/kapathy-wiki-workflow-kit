## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Evidence-coordinate heading system

The Stripe AI company evolution H5 SHALL use a unified evidence-coordinate
heading system instead of conversational or rhetorical headings.

#### Scenario: Reader scans section titles

- **WHEN** a reader scans the H5 section headings
- **THEN** major sections use `证据坐标` naming or equivalent concise report
  taxonomy
- **AND** headings such as `Stripe 是否已经接近系统级入口？`,
  `三维时间轴：时间 x 证据维度 x 证明任务`, `词条密度：技术、商业、金融、风险四类信号`,
  and `并购不是大事列表：它们补的是能力缺口` do not appear

### Requirement: Interactive signal index

The Stripe AI company evolution H5 SHALL make the signal cloud interactive and
evidence-bearing.

#### Scenario: Reader clicks a signal term

- **WHEN** a reader clicks a signal term
- **THEN** the H5 updates a detail panel for that term
- **AND** the detail panel shows the signal category, what it proves, source
  route or count, and evidence boundary

### Requirement: Source inventory scale

The Stripe AI company evolution H5 SHALL distinguish high-level proof nodes
from the 1000-source queue.

#### Scenario: Reader reviews evidence scale

- **WHEN** a reader reaches the evidence boundary section
- **THEN** the page shows grouped source inventory counts
- **AND** it states that coordinate nodes are proof nodes, not the full source
  count
- **AND** it does not claim all 1000 source rows have been fully read
