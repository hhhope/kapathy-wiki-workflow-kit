## Why

The repository now has accepted governance surfaces for parts of
`agents-governance-drift`, but it still also has multiple candidate materials:

- distill
- playbook
- calibration pages

Those pages remain useful as historical calibration evidence, but if their
relationship to the accepted mainline stays implicit, the topic is not truly
closed.

## What Changes

- Define the accepted relationship between candidate materials and the accepted
  governance mainline.
- Decide which candidate pages remain as historical evidence, which need
  stronger downgrade wording, and which need cross-links to accepted artifacts.
- Remove ambiguity about whether candidate pages are planning documents or just
  preserved calibration history.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `agents-governance-candidate-closure`: The repository SHALL explicitly relate
  `agents-governance-drift` candidate materials to the accepted mainline before
  treating the topic as closed.

### Modified Capabilities

- `repo-agents-governance-phase0`
- `disagreement-management-method`

## Impact

- Adds OpenSpec artifacts under
  `openspec/changes/close-agents-governance-candidate-materials/`.
- Creates the closure boundary between accepted governance and candidate
  historical calibration pages.
