# Refine Stripe H5 Strategy Topology And Timeline Density

## Why

The current Stripe H5 still treats acquisitions as a standalone chapter. The
user clarified that acquisitions should be read inside the company evolution
timeline and the comprehensive strategy view: commercial actions should show how
they affected technical choices, and technical choices should show which
commercial moves they enabled.

The strategy chapter has the same problem: it lists strategic choices but does
not show whether those choices are sequential, parallel, or cross-reused. The
reader needs to see a main strategic trunk, horizontal capability fill-ins, and
AI-era recombination.

The comprehensive view also has too few nodes for the research density. Twenty
two nodes makes the atlas feel under-sampled relative to the source queue.

## What Changes

- Replace the seven-row strategy reading with a strategy topology timeline:
  main trunk, horizontal capability lines, and AI-era cross-reuse.
- Merge acquisition / capability-gap examples into the company evolution
  timeline as line / scatter nodes, not standalone cards.
- Reframe the comprehensive view as `商业动作 x 技术选择` rather than a separate
  capability map.
- Expand the interactive node set from 22 to at least 36 nodes across company,
  product, finance, risk, agent / protocol, and organization signals.
- Preserve source-boundary language and internal-draft status.

## Non-Goals

- No new source collection.
- No claim that all 1000 queued URLs have been fully read.
- No Feishu sync or public publication claim.
- No unrelated report or workflow edits.
