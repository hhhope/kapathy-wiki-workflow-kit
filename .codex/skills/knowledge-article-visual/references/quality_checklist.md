# Quality Checklist

## Article

- Core thesis is clear
- Four sections are progressive
- Each section has one clear claim
- Examples are concrete
- Ending summarizes the SOP

## Visuals

- 16:9 layout
- Warm paper background
- Hand-drawn style
- No text overlap
- Chinese text readable
- Cards aligned
- Same color system across images
- Icons support the text
- Bottom takeaway exists

## Regenerate If

- Chinese is broken
- Text overlaps
- Image looks like PPT
- Style is inconsistent
- Too much blank space
- Too crowded
- Key point is unclear
