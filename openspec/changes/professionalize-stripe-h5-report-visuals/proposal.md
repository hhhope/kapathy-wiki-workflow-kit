# Professionalize Stripe H5 Report Visuals

## Why

The Stripe H5 still reads too much like an agent explaining its work rather than
a professional business-analysis report. The latest user review flags concrete
failures:

- transaction / credit rails are conceptually right but visually and verbally
  unprofessional
- the `1000 条 queue` pill is too conversational
- the first-screen volume title cites a conclusion without a visible source
  boundary
- the timeline heading is strong, but the supporting copy falls back to
  explanatory process language
- `证据维度` is unclear and forces clicking instead of using a clear visual
  encoding
- the AI payment restructuring section still sounds like machine-generated AI
  commentary rather than industry analysis
- quote cards should explain the analytical purpose of citations and use simple
  gray quote blocks
- the signal matrix still resembles a word cloud

## What Changes

- Replace transaction / credit rail copy with payment-industry terms and a
  cleaner two-row operating-flow visual.
- Replace conversational meta copy with evidence-pool wording.
- Make the volume title source-aware and avoid implying an unsupported source
  claim in the headline.
- Rewrite timeline support copy into a compact business-analysis premise.
- Replace `证据维度` with visual encoding labels that the reader can understand
  without clicking.
- Rewrite the AI payment restructuring section as a market-structure analysis:
  distribution, credentials, merchant systems, settlement rails, risk and credit.
- Simplify source quotes into gray quote blocks with short analytical framing.
- Replace the signal matrix / word-cloud surface with grouped evidence bars or
  lanes.
- Refresh screenshots and record verification.

## Non-Goals

- No new source collection.
- No Feishu sync or public release.
- No full redesign of the lower interactive capability map.
- No edits to unrelated report pages or workflow rules.

## Impact

This is a focused report-visual and report-language repair for the Stripe H5 and
its gate-review evidence.
