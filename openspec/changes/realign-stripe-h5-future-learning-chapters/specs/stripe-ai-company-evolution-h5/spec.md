## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Stripe H5 future judgment and learning chapter split

The Stripe AI company evolution H5 SHALL separate the future judgment chapter
from the final learning synthesis chapter.

#### Scenario: Reader reviews future judgment

- **WHEN** a reader reaches the post-competition / synthesis portion of the H5
- **THEN** the page SHALL include a Chapter 12 future-judgment section
- **AND** the section SHALL use historical development patterns to locate the
  current AI-payment stage and the next five-year path
- **AND** the section SHALL include an L1-L4 maturity model covering information
  assistance, task execution, limited transactions, and auditable financial
  execution
- **AND** it SHALL mark the current stage as around `2026 / L2.5`.

#### Scenario: Reader interprets the future visual

- **WHEN** the Chapter 12 future-judgment visual is shown
- **THEN** it SHALL be a folded line-chart or equivalent line-based stage chart
- **AND** it SHALL show 2024-2031 on the time axis
- **AND** it SHALL separate technical integration, authorization / liability,
  regulatory acceptance, and commercial outcome maturity.

#### Scenario: Reader reviews protocol meaning

- **WHEN** the H5 explains AP2, MPP, x402, or related machine-payment protocols
- **THEN** the page SHALL state that these are human-defined rule and protocol
  systems that agents follow
- **AND** the page SHALL not imply that agents become independent legal or
  financial subjects.

#### Scenario: Reader reviews final learning

- **WHEN** the reader reaches the final synthesis
- **THEN** the page SHALL include Chapter 13 `我们能学什么`
- **AND** the chapter SHALL explain that AI lowers implementation cost while
  raising the cost of financial design, evaluation, audit, liability, consumer
  protection, risk, settlement, and dispute boundaries
- **AND** it SHALL conclude that Stripe's lesson is compounding
  standardization: turning fragmented transaction and credit relationships into
  objects, APIs, pricing, authorization rules, risk controls, settlement flows,
  and ecosystem packages.

#### Scenario: Repair verification is recorded

- **WHEN** this repair is completed
- **THEN** verification SHALL include static checks for the required chapter
  headings and L1-L4 labels
- **AND** desktop and mobile screenshots SHALL be read back for the new line
  chart and final learning chapter
- **AND** keyword presence alone SHALL NOT count as structure verification.
