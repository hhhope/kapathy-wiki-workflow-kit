# account-group-onboarding-h5 Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: AI Collaboration Starts From Account-Group Offerings

The H5 SHALL explain what the account group can provide to AI collaboration
before describing AI tools or workflow names.

#### Scenario: AI section is account-domain first

- **WHEN** a reviewer reads `AI 如何与我们协作`
- **THEN** the section explains that the account group provides account-domain
  maps, Account Harness constraints, source material, verification evidence,
  and human review boundaries
- **AND** OpenSpec, Lore, Graphify, and lore-bootstrap are presented as
  supporting mechanisms rather than the primary value proposition.

### Requirement: Visual Style Fits Internal Technical Training

The H5 SHALL use a calm technical-training visual style rather than a rough
heavy card/poster style.

#### Scenario: style is reviewable

- **WHEN** a reviewer opens the H5 on desktop or mobile
- **THEN** the page uses lighter section borders, restrained shadows, consistent
  card hierarchy, readable spacing, and existing source-backed diagrams as the
  primary visual evidence
- **AND** repeated thick black card outlines and graph-paper background noise do
  not dominate the reading experience.
