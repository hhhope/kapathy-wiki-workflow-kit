# Proposal: Refine Account H5 AI Offering And Style

## Why

The current account-group onboarding H5 still reads like a rough internal
card stack. Its visual system is heavy and noisy, and the AI collaboration
section describes tools before explaining what the account group can provide:
account-domain context, harness constraints, verification evidence, and
engineering boundaries.

## What Changes

- Reframe `AI 如何与我们协作` around what the account group provides to AI and
  newcomers: domain maps, Account Harness constraints, source material,
  verification evidence, and review boundaries.
- Keep AI tools such as OpenSpec, Lore, Graphify, and lore-bootstrap as
  supporting mechanisms instead of the main message.
- Replace the rough poster/card-heavy visual style with a calmer technical
  training style:
  - cleaner page background;
  - lighter section containers;
  - more disciplined card hierarchy;
  - reduced thick borders and shadow noise;
  - improved section spacing and mobile readability.
- Update the gate review with the new drift, visual decision, and verification
  evidence.

## Out of Scope

- Feishu sync or image insertion.
- Adding new frontend dependencies.
- Changing source diagrams beyond embedding and page-level presentation.
- Changing account-domain facts outside the H5 repair scope.
- Introducing credentials, internal URLs, personal contacts, QR codes, or
  access instructions.

## Impact

- The existing H5 is edited in place.
- Existing visual assets remain local assets; the repair changes page style and
  AI section framing.
- The gate review records that the previous style and AI framing were not
  acceptable.
