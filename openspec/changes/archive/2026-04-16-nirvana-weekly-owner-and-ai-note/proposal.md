## Why

The current Nirvana weekly records already capture weekly progress and follow-up items, but two clarified weekly facts still need to be reflected:

- the first-line owner for current alignment is the user
- the current week's AI-efficiency conclusion is "installation"

Without recording these details, the weekly reminder still leaves the owner implicit and the AI item remains vague.

## What Changes

- Update the existing Nirvana weekly intake page with the clarified AI-efficiency note.
- Update the existing Nirvana weekly reminder page with the explicit first-line owner and AI-efficiency wording.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `nirvana-weekly-view-sync`: The weekly Nirvana records now preserve explicit owner and AI-efficiency clarification for the current week.

## Impact

- Adds OpenSpec artifacts under `openspec/changes/nirvana-weekly-owner-and-ai-note/`.
- Updates the existing weekly intake and reminder pages only.
