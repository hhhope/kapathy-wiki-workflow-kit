## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Strategy topology visual

The Stripe AI company evolution H5 SHALL show how Stripe's strategic choices
relate over time instead of presenting them as parallel product rows.

#### Scenario: Reader reviews strategic causality

- **WHEN** the reader reaches `哪些战略造就了 Stripe`
- **THEN** the H5 SHALL include a strategy topology timeline or equivalent
  relationship visual
- **AND** the visual SHALL distinguish main-trunk progression from horizontal
  capability fill-ins and AI-era cross-reuse
- **AND** the visual SHALL include the labels `主干递进`, `横向补位`, and
  `AI 复用`
- **AND** the H5 SHALL not present the strategies only as a flat list or
  parallel card grid.

### Requirement: Timeline-integrated acquisition evidence

The Stripe AI company evolution H5 SHALL present acquisitions as timeline
evidence, not as a standalone acquisition chapter or card lane.

#### Scenario: Reader sees acquisition context in the timeline

- **WHEN** the reader reviews the company evolution timeline
- **THEN** acquisition and capability-gap events SHALL appear inside the
  timeline context as line / scatter nodes
- **AND** each event SHALL state the capability gap it filled
- **AND** each event SHALL connect to a later technical or AI-era reuse path
- **AND** the H5 SHALL not show `并购 / 能力补位 lane` as a standalone section.

### Requirement: Comprehensive strategy node density

The Stripe AI company evolution H5 SHALL provide a comprehensive strategy view
with enough evidence nodes to avoid an under-sampled atlas.

#### Scenario: Reader opens the comprehensive map

- **WHEN** the comprehensive strategy map renders
- **THEN** it SHALL contain at least 36 nodes across company, product, finance,
  risk, agent / protocol, and organization signals.
