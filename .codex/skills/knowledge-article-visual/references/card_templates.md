# Card Templates

## Template 1: Four-Step Flow Card

Use for core experience or process explanation.

Structure:

- Title
- Subtitle
- Old vs new comparison banner
- 4 step cards across the middle
- Bottom takeaway

## Template 2: Three-Layer Structure Card

Use for architecture or capability structure.

Structure:

- Title
- Subtitle
- 3 large cards across the middle
- Arrows between cards
- Bottom takeaway

## Template 3: Product Iteration Card

Use for version evolution.

Structure:

- Title
- Subtitle
- Decision banner
- Two side-by-side examples
- Version cards under each example
- Bottom takeaway

## Template 4: Two-Scenario Thinking Card

Use for principle explanation.

Structure:

- Title
- Subtitle
- Center ribbon
- Two large panels
- Two small examples
- Bottom takeaway
