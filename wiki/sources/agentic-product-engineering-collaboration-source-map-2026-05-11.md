## Research Goal

Build a management-facing view of how AI agents are changing product and
engineering collaboration across the software delivery lifecycle.

Primary decision target: support a 30-60 day pilot for agent-assisted product
engineering workflows.

Second-pass correction on 2026-05-11: the source set must cover the company
operating model, organization design, roles, and people model, not only tooling.
The user-provided WeChat article is treated as a secondary synthesis and claim
router, not as the source of record for PayPal or Keith Rabois claims.

## Emerging Patterns

1. The unit of work is moving from prompt/session to issue, ticket, PR, spec,
   workflow object, or business-operation object.
2. Agent work needs a control plane: Jira, Linear, GitHub Issues, repository
   plans, IDE/platform orchestration, finance operations queues, or internal
   workflow systems.
3. Product, design, operations, data, legal, marketing, support, and finance can
   initiate more work, which raises the value of judgment, acceptance criteria,
   review packets, and explicit "do not do" boundaries.
4. The scarce human role is not "person who types code"; it is the person who
   can take an idea from intent to shipped outcome while using agents as
   leverage. Keith Rabois calls this a barrel; the WeChat article maps it to
   OPD. This is a candidate local people model, not yet an adopted rule.
5. Engineering leadership shifts from "who writes code" to "who designs the
   harness, guardrails, repo knowledge, tests, and review system."
6. CEO and HR-level materials show AI moving into headcount, performance review,
   hiring, contractor strategy, and organization design. This means the report
   cannot stay at tool adoption.
7. Mature examples converge on governance: permissions, auditability, cost
   attribution, branch protections, CI gates, sandboxing, evals, production
   isolation, and observability.
8. Research sources temper vendor claims: AI adoption can improve throughput but
   also increase debugging load, delivery instability, toolchain sprawl, and
   trust gaps.

## Skip / Caution

- Vendor metrics are useful signals, not neutral benchmarks.
- Product marketing pages are treated as evidence of vendor strategy, not proof
  that every customer achieves the claimed workflow.
- The final report should avoid claiming that agent-first workflows generalize
  without similar investment in tests, repo knowledge, guardrails, and review.
- WeChat and podcast summaries can route claims, but final hard claims should
  cite the upstream interview, memo, report, or company page.
- Headcount reduction cases must be handled carefully: "AI enables smaller
  teams" and "AI is used to justify cuts" are different claims.
