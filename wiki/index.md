# Report Wiki Index

This wiki organizes reporting knowledge across four stable views so the same material can be reused without copy-paste.

## Entry Views

- [Domains](domains/index.md): business topics and thematic knowledge pages
- [Reports](reports/index.md): audience-oriented report pages and draft outputs
- [Timeline](timeline/index.md): period-based review and archive pages
- [Sources](sources/index.md): Feishu and other upstream evidence pages

## Recommended Flow

1. Register upstream material under `Sources`.
2. Link or summarize it into the relevant `Domains`.
3. Reuse those links inside `Timeline` pages for each reporting period.
4. Assemble audience-ready drafts under `Reports`.

## Governance Aids

- [Role Boundaries](role-boundaries.md): decide whether a page serves project delivery or team reuse
- [Team Lore Candidates](team-lore-candidates.md): stage reusable content before promoting it to team lore
