## Context

The repository intentionally preserved candidate and calibration materials
instead of flattening them too early. That was the right method decision.

But once accepted governance surfaces exist, those preserved candidate pages
need a closure relationship:

- historical evidence
- superseded planning draft
- still-open branch
- or mixed role with explicit boundaries

Without that closure work, later readers can still mistake candidate pages for
live planning documents.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals**

- Classify the surviving candidate pages by their enduring role.
- Cross-link accepted mainline artifacts and candidate history.
- Reduce ambiguity without erasing the calibration trail.

**Non-Goals**

- Do not delete history just to make the wiki look cleaner.
- Do not redo the whole governance exploration.
- Do not claim that candidate material had no value.

## Decisions To Resolve

### Decision: Candidate preservation model

Candidate pages may need one of:

- keep as historical calibration evidence
- keep with stronger downgrade wording
- archive/retire from main navigation

### Decision: Accepted-mainline cross-link model

Readers should be able to tell from the candidate page itself what accepted
artifact now owns the mainline, if any.
